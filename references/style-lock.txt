Create one original 16:9 editorial explainer illustration on elegant warm aged parchment. Use a faint low-contrast grid, subtle paper fibers and creases, a fine double-line border, and restrained ornamental corners. Build the central scene from dimensional paper-cut and paper-craft objects with slightly irregular edges, natural wear, and consistent short soft shadows.

Use a centered editorial title hierarchy at the top: a small eyebrow, one large high-contrast headline, and one concise terracotta or ink-brown subheadline. The main paper-craft tableau occupies the middle and lower canvas. Add only 2–4 compact paper labels with short leader lines and small target dots, plus an optional bottom takeaway ribbon. Keep every word, label, object, road, card, and ornament fully inside a generous safe area.

Use dark ink brown, terracotta, dusty indigo, muted sage, mustard ochre, and warm brick red. The image should feel premium, historical-documentary, tactile, calm, and easy to understand. People are optional; use them only when they improve the story.

Do not make a PPT slide, corporate vector infographic, white-background sketch, dense academic diagram, dashboard, game-like 3D render, children's illustration, or realistic photograph. Do not crop text or objects. Do not let labels cover the subject. Do not add any text beyond the exact requested strings.
